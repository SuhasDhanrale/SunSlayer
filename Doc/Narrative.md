That is a beautiful and deeply resonant twist! Tying the run to saving his cursed sister on his back immediately solves both the why and the urgency of the game. It’s emotional, high-stakes, and gives a very clear purpose to the endless running.

Let's unpack how we can weave this into the lore and the gameplay:

1. The Core Narrative (The "Why")
The Sister: She has been infected by a powerful demon curse. It is slowly spreading through her body. You are carrying her on your back, either wrapped in a sacred cloth or protected in a traditional traveling chest.
The Goal: You need to reach the Grand Torii of the Sun Goddess (Amaterasu) at the peak of the sacred volcanic mountain. Only the first rays of dawn at that shrine can burn away the curse and save her.
The Urgency: The curse is a ticking clock. If you stop running, or if the shadows catch you, the curse consumes her completely.
2. The Chase (The "What is happening")
The Pursuers: The demon lord who cursed her (our Boss) does not want the curse lifted. He sends his shadow spirits to chase you and block your path.
Why we are slicing: The forest itself is being manipulated by the demon lord. Bamboo is shooting up rapidly out of the ground, and old temple ruins are collapsing to block your path. Slicing through these bamboo fences and stone pillars isn't just about clearing a path—it is about maintaining momentum. If you hit an obstacle, you slow down, and the chasing boss closes the gap to grab your sister.
3. How the Intro Scene Plays Out (The Hook)
Visual Concept: We start in a quiet, traditional Japanese room. The sister is lying on a futon, breathing heavily, with glowing purple veins of corruption spreading across her face.
The Inciting Incident: Suddenly, the shoji screen doors shatter. Red, glowing demon eyes appear in the darkness outside. The Monk immediately pulls his sister onto his back, ties a rope around them, and draws his katana.
The Leadoff: He rolls out of the sliding door just as a shadow claw smashes the room, landing on the bamboo path. He focuses his stance, draws his blade, and the sprint begins.
4. How the Biomes Tell the Story (The Journey)
As you run, the biomes trace your journey up the mountain to save her:

Rainy Forest: Sprinting through the dark foothills of the mountain in a storm, escaping the initial ambush.
Sunset Fireflies: Dusk is falling, and you are navigating the dense bamboo groves, realizing time is running out.
Snowy Shacks: High-altitude mountain pass in the dead of winter. The cold slows down the curse, but the terrain is treacherous.
Volcanic Danger (The Peak): You've reached the active volcanic peak where the Sun Shrine resides. Lava flows and ash fall everywhere as the Boss makes his final, desperate stand to stop you.
